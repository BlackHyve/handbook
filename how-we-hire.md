# How we hire

We respect candidates' time. We want to understand work you've actually shipped, the decisions behind it, and what it's like to work through a real problem together.

Our [open roles](README.md#open-roles) list shows what we're currently hiring for. Some job descriptions remain in the handbook for reference and are marked as closed. The job description for an open role explains what to send to **apply@blackhyve.com** and the process for that role.

## Product Designers

For our Principal Product Designer role, the process is:

1. **Application review.** We look closely at the work you send and the thinking behind it.
2. **Conversation and past-work deep dive.** About an hour. We'll talk about your work, BlackHyve, and the design problems we're trying to solve. You'll walk us through one or two substantial projects, including the messy parts: constraints, alternatives, disagreements, tradeoffs, and what actually shipped.
3. **Work through a real BlackHyve problem together.** Rather than give you a speculative design exercise, we'll open up an actual part of the product and talk through it together. We're interested in how you see the problem, what you notice, and how you collaborate.
4. **Meet more of the team.** You'll talk with the engineers and people you'd work closely with.
5. **References and next steps.** We talk to the references you sent us and, if there's a fit, figure out the right way to start working together.

No design homework. No unpaid take-home project. We'd rather spend time looking at work you've actually done and solve a real problem together.

We're open to fractional, part-time, or full-time depending on the person. Finding the right fit matters more than prescribing the arrangement in advance. Prior AI experience isn't a hiring requirement; we can teach AI workflows to someone with exceptional design taste and product judgment.

See the [Principal Product Designer job description](jd-principal-product-designer.md) for the application details and engagement options.

## Product Engineers

Our engineer roles are currently closed. The process below describes the approach in the reference job descriptions:

1. **Application review.** We read what you sent: the cover letter, the ship links, the references. If there's a fit, we'll reach out within a week.
2. **Conversation with the CTO.** About 45 minutes. We dig into your work, your motivations, and your read on the problem space.
3. **Deep-dive on past work.** You walk us through one or two things you've shipped: the decisions, the tradeoffs, what you'd do differently.
4. **Join a working session.** Sit in on one of our team huddles and/or a shaping session and work through a real problem with us. We're not testing your code; we're seeing how you think about a problem.
5. **Conversation with the CEO.** About 45 minutes. Customer empathy, business fit, mutual gut-check.
6. **References and offer.** We talk to two or three of the people you sent us. If we're a fit, we make an offer.

Usually 2–3 weeks end-to-end.
