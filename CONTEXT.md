# Context: BlackHyve Handbook

A glossary of the terms we use in our hiring, leveling, and handbook docs. Definitions only — no implementation details, no specs.

## Glossary

### Product Engineer
Our single engineering ladder (Junior → Product → Senior → Lead → Principal). A Product Engineer ships product end-to-end: writes code, shapes the problem, talks to customers, and operates it in production. AI-native by default. Defined in [Titles for Product Engineers](titles-for-product-engineers.md).

### Platform (as a role focus)
A *focus label* applied to a Product Engineer role — **not** a separate title, ladder, or comp track. "Platform" narrows what the person primarily owns (integrations) without fencing them off from customer-facing product work. The same ladder and comp bands apply, which preserves the flexibility to move them onto product when priorities shift. Distinct from a "Platform Engineer" track, which BlackHyve deliberately chose *not* to create (avoids new leveling/comp overhead).

### Integrations
The primary area of ownership for the Platform-focus role. Scope is two-sided: **inbound** ingestion of customer data into BlackHyve, and **outbound** connections to the systems construction teams already run. Priority targets, in order:
- **Project-management ERPs / accounting (the main goal):** Foundation, Trimble Vista, Trimble Spectrum, Sage 300, COINS.
- **Construction platforms:** Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud.
- **Scheduling tools:** lower priority for now.

The strategic aim is to make connecting BlackHyve to these systems *dead simple* — a precursor to a possible BlackHyve integrations **marketplace**.

### Marketplace
A future state where integrations are self-serve/productized rather than bespoke. Not the job today, but the direction the integrations work is meant to point toward. Aspirational — frames the "why this matters" of the Platform role.
