# Compensation

## Base salary bands

### Product Engineers

| Level | US Band |
|---|---|
| Junior Product Engineer | $60,000–$90,000 |
| Product Engineer | $75,000–$110,000 |
| Senior Product Engineer | $95,000–$130,000 |
| Lead Product Engineer | $135,000–$185,000 |
| Principal Product Engineer | $145,000–$225,000 |

Bands apply to annualized base salary. Level criteria are in [Titles for Product Engineers](titles-for-product-engineers.md).

### Product Designers

| Level | US Band |
|---|---|
| Junior Product Designer | $55,000–$80,000 |
| Product Designer | $70,000–$100,000 |
| Senior Product Designer | $85,000–$115,000 |
| Lead Product Designer | $120,000–$165,000 |
| Principal Product Designer | $130,000–$205,000 |

Bands apply to annualized full-time base salary. Level criteria are in [Titles for Product Designers](titles-for-designers.md). We use the same five levels and location modifiers as engineering, with designer bands set at roughly 90% of the corresponding engineering bands, rounded to $5,000 increments.

For part-time employment, salary is prorated to the agreed working schedule. Fractional consulting fees are agreed separately based on scope, time commitment, and engagement terms; the employee salary bands aren't contractor rate cards. We discuss compensation early in the process.

#### Market context

As of October 7, 2026, Levels.fyi reports US median total compensation of [$172,000 for Product Designers](https://www.levels.fyi/t/product-designer/locations/united-states) and [$196,500 for Software Engineers](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/united-states), putting design at about 88% of engineering. These figures include equity and bonus and combine career levels; they aren't base-salary benchmarks for individual levels.

For a broader wage comparison, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reports May 2025 median annual wages of [$104,000 for web and digital interface designers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/web-developers.htm) and [$135,980 for software developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm). Those occupations cover more than SaaS product teams and don't map directly to our career ladders.

We use these comparisons as context for the relationship between the two ladders. The roughly 90% relationship is our band-setting choice, anchored to BlackHyve's existing engineering bands, rather than a claim that these sources establish exact market salaries at each level. These bands incorporate our existing cash compensation philosophy.

## Location modifier

| Locations | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| US; Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal | 1.00 |
| Other Canada; Western Europe | 0.85 |
| Rest of world | 0.60 |

The modifier applies to US base salary at every level in both ladders. New locations are added as we hire into them.

## Equity

Equity is part of the conversation for offers at BlackHyve. Grants are structured individually at offer time. Ask about it during the process — we'd rather discuss it early than have it be a surprise.

## Reviews

We review bands annually and as the company scales. Cash bands today sit roughly 25% below comparable AI-pilled remote SaaS shops; we expect that gap to close as the company grows.
